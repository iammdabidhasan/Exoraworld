How We Collect Data

We collect data through the following methods:

  • Direct Interaction: When you create an account, place an order, submit payment proof, fill out a contact form, or communicate with us directly
  • Firebase Authentication: When you sign in via Google or Facebook, Firebase transmits your name, email, and profile picture to ExoraWorld. Firebase also assigns a unique user ID (UID) that we use to associate your data with your account
  • Automated Tracking: When you browse ExoraWorld, we automatically collect technical and behavioral data via analytics tools, cookies, and server-side logging
  • Firestore (Firebase Database): For authenticated users, the gallery section stores favorites, recently viewed items, and view counts in Firebase Firestore, associated with your user account
  • Third-Party Tools: Analytics platforms (e.g. Google Analytics), advertising networks (e.g. Google AdSense), and other integrated services may collect data about your visit independently under their own privacy policies
  • Publicly Available Information: In some contexts, we may reference publicly available information about users or visitors as part of fraud prevention or security operations

How We Use Your Data

We use the data we collect for the following legitimate purposes:

  • Order Processing & Delivery: To verify payments, fulfill digital product orders, and deliver products to the contact information you provided
  • Account Management: To create and maintain your ExoraWorld account, authenticate you via Firebase, and provide account-specific features (gallery favorites, game history, etc.)
  • Customer Support: To respond to your inquiries, resolve disputes, and provide assistance with orders or platform issues
  • Platform Operation: To run, maintain, secure, and improve every section of ExoraWorld — including the gallery, news hub, games, store, and content sections
  • Analytics & Insights: To understand how users interact with our platform and improve user experience and content quality
  • Advertising: To serve personalized and non-personalized advertisements through integrated advertising networks
  • Communications: To send you transactional messages (order confirmations, delivery notifications) and, where you have opted in, platform updates and newsletters
  • Legal Compliance & Fraud Prevention: To detect, investigate, and prevent fraudulent transactions, policy violations, and illegal activities
  • Security: To protect the platform, our users, and our business from unauthorized access, abuse, and threats

News Aggregation & Privacy

ExoraWorld's News section aggregates publicly available news headlines and brief descriptions from RSS feeds published by BBC News, IGN, The Daily Star Bangladesh, and other media organizations via the rss2json.com API.

  • No User Data Sent to Publishers: When ExoraWorld fetches news content from RSS feeds, we do not transmit any personally identifiable user data to those news publishers
  • rss2json.com: This API service processes our RSS fetch requests and may log our server-side API calls. Users are not individually tracked by rss2json.com through ExoraWorld's news feature
  • External Links: Clicking a news article link takes you to the original publisher's website. Once you leave ExoraWorld, that publisher's privacy policy applies. We have no control over their data practices
  • No News Content Stored: We do not store or cache news article text in our own database. News content is fetched in real-time and displayed temporarily

Gallery Content & Privacy

ExoraWorld's Gallery (Lumina Gallery) hosts original photography by the platform owner and curated public domain / Creative Commons imagery. The gallery includes a Firebase-backed user feature set for authenticated users.

  • Favorites & View History: Authenticated users can favorite images and track recently viewed items. This data is stored in Firebase Firestore under your user account and is not shared with third parties
  • View Counts: Aggregate view counts are tracked per image for internal analytics. These are not linked to personally identifiable information
  • Image Downloads: When you download an image, we may log the download event (image name and timestamp) to understand gallery usage. We do not log your identity in connection with individual downloads unless you are signed in
  • Local Storage: For anonymous users, gallery favorites are temporarily stored in your browser's localStorage. If you later sign in, this local data is merged into your Firebase account

Cookies & Tracking Technologies

ExoraWorld uses the following types of cookies and tracking technologies:

  • Session Cookies: Temporary cookies that expire when you close your browser. Used to maintain your session state and preferences during a visit
  • Persistent Cookies: Cookies that remain on your device for a set period. Used to remember your theme preference (light/dark mode) and other settings
  • localStorage: Browser local storage is used to temporarily store gallery favorites for anonymous users and your theme override preference
  • sessionStorage: Used to store your manual theme selection so it persists across pages within a single browsing session without reverting to the auto-time-based theme
  • Analytics Cookies: Placed by Google Analytics and similar services to track page visits, session duration, user flows, and platform performance. These cookies are subject to Google's Privacy Policy
  • Advertising Cookies: Placed by Google AdSense, Meta Audience Network, and other advertising networks to enable personalized advertising based on your browsing behavior
  • Firebase Cookies: Firebase Authentication uses cookies and tokens to maintain your logged-in session on ExoraWorld

You can control and manage cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect certain features of ExoraWorld, including account login and gallery favorites. Our Cookie Policy (if separate) provides additional detail on cookie management.

Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, or as required by applicable law. Specific retention periods:

  • Order Records: Retained for 2 years from the date of order for financial record-keeping, dispute resolution, and legal compliance
  • Payment Proof Submissions: Retained for 2 years from the date of submission
  • Account Data (Firebase): Retained for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your Firebase authentication record and associated Firestore data are deleted, subject to any legal retention requirements
  • Analytics Data: Aggregated analytics data retained for up to 12 months. Google Analytics retains data per their own retention settings, which you can adjust in your Google account
  • Communication Data: Messages and support correspondence retained for up to 2 years for reference and legal purposes
  • Cookie Data: Session cookies expire when you close your browser. Persistent cookies expire per their individual expiry periods (typically 30–365 days)

You may request deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us. Certain data may be retained beyond your deletion request where required by law or for legitimate fraud prevention purposes.

Data Security

We take reasonable and appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These measures include:

  • Firebase Security: User authentication and Firestore database access is protected by Firebase security rules and Google's enterprise-grade infrastructure

  • Payment Security: ExoraWorld does not store your payment credentials, card numbers, or account passwords. Payment transactions are handled entirely by the respective payment service providers (Bkash, Nagad, etc.)
  • Fraud Detection: We monitor for suspicious activity such as fraudulent payment proof submissions and take appropriate action including account suspension

Important Limitation: No method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security. In the event of a data breach that affects your rights or freedoms, we will notify affected users as required by applicable law, within 72 hours of becoming aware of the breach.
