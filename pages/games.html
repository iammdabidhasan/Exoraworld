Guide

How to Play

⬆ JUMP
SPACE or ↑ — Tap again mid-air for a double jump.
⬇ SLIDE
↓ or Shift — Duck under high obstacles.
💥 BLAST
R — Fire a shockwave that destroys all obstacles on screen. 3s cooldown.
✦ COINS
Collect coins for points. Build combos for multiplied scoring up to ×8.
❤️ LIVES
You have 3 lives. Hit an obstacle to lose one. Shield absorbs a hit automatically.
🔊 SOUND
M — Toggle music and sound effects on/off.
Power-Ups
🛡️ Shield — Blocks one hit 🚀 Fly — Float above obstacles 🧲 Magnet — Pulls nearby coins ⚡ ×2 Score — Double points 🔵 Tiny — Smaller hitbox 💥 Blast — Instant shockwave
