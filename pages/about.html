Our Mission

Built because scattered apps are exhausting.

ExoraWorld started with one frustration: the tools you use, the games you play, the news and content you follow, and the store you shop from were all spread across a dozen different apps. Switching between them felt like a second job.

So we built one home for all of it. A platform where you can browse a gallery of original photography, read live world and gaming news, play browser games, buy original digital products, and read fresh content — without ever leaving.

Every product in the store is designed for real use. Every game on the platform is hand-built. Every photo in the gallery is either personally photographed or sourced from verified public domain collections. And every news item links straight back to the original source.

Our Values

What we stand for

The principles that guide every decision we make, every feature we build, and every piece of content we publish.

Speed by Default

Every page on ExoraWorld is optimized for fast load times. We minimize bloat, use modern web standards, and constantly refine the experience so you spend less time waiting and more time doing.

Security by Design

Your account, data, and payments are protected at every step. We use Firebase Authentication for secure sign-in, HTTPS for all data transmission, and clear policies about exactly who can access your information and why.

Truly Global

ExoraWorld is built from Bangladesh but designed for the world. Our members span 80+ countries, our payment methods include both local (Bkash, Nagad) and international (AirTM, Skrill, Payoneer) options, and our content covers global topics.

Originality Always

From our games and store products to our gallery photography and written content — we create or carefully source everything. We don't copy, we don't rebrand, and we're fully transparent about where every piece of content comes from.

Always Improving

New features, new games, new products, and new content ship every single month. ExoraWorld is not a static platform — it grows with its community. If you have feedback, it genuinely shapes what gets built next.

Real People, Real Answers

When you contact ExoraWorld — for support, a dispute, or a copyright question — a real person reads and responds to your message. No bots, no canned form-letter replies. We treat every user the way we'd want to be treated.

Our Story

How ExoraWorld came to be

From a frustration with scattered apps to a fully featured all-in-one digital platform.

The Idea

The frustration that started it all

MD Abid Hasan wanted one place for tools, games, news, and content — and couldn't find it. So he decided to build it. ExoraWorld was conceived as a fully independent, all-in-one digital world operated from Bangladesh.

Foundation

The platform is born

ExoraWorld launches with its core sections: the Gallery, News Hub, and Content page. Firebase Authentication is integrated for secure Google and Facebook sign-in. The first original digital products appear in the Store.

Games

VoidRunner X launches

ExoraWorld releases its first fully original browser game — VoidRunner X. Built entirely in-house with no third-party game engines or licensed assets, it marks ExoraWorld's entry into original software development and becomes the flagship game of the platform.

Growth

Community crosses 50,000 members

The ExoraWorld community grows to 50,000+ members across 80+ countries. The store expands with new templates and tools. Live news aggregation from BBC, IGN, and The Daily Star goes live, powered by open-source RSS feeds parsed via rss2json.com.

Today

One world, always growing

ExoraWorld continues to expand — new games in development, new store products launching, new gallery collections added regularly, and fresh content published constantly. The mission stays the same: one home for everything you love online.
