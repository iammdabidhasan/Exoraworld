01

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by websites you visit. They serve many purposes: keeping you logged in, remembering your preferences, measuring how the site is used, and enabling advertising.

ExoraWorld uses standard browser cookies alongside two additional browser storage technologies — localStorage and sessionStorage — which work similarly to cookies but stay local to your browser and are never transmitted to a server with each request.

Plain language: Cookies let us remember who you are between page loads (your login), what you prefer (dark mode), and what you've done (gallery favorites). Some are set by us directly; others are set by Google and advertising partners whose code runs on the page.

04

Browser Storage (localStorage & sessionStorage)

Alongside cookies, ExoraWorld uses two browser-native storage mechanisms. Unlike cookies, these are never sent to our server — they exist only in your browser.

sessionStorage

Used for one key: exo_theme_ovr. When you manually toggle the light/dark theme, your choice is saved here with a 1-hour expiry so it persists as you navigate across pages. When the session ends or the hour expires, ExoraWorld returns to automatically choosing the theme based on your local time (light 6am–7pm, dark otherwise).

localStorage

Used to store gallery favorites for users who are not signed in. This means you can heart images and they'll still be there when you come back — even without an account. When you sign in, this local data is merged into your Firebase account so nothing is lost.

How to clear browser storage: Open your browser's DevTools (F12), go to the Application tab, then Storage → localStorage / sessionStorage, and select the ExoraWorld origin to delete entries individually or clear all. Alternatively, clearing your browser's site data from settings removes everything at once.

Third-Party Cookies & Services

ExoraWorld integrates third-party services that place their own cookies. We do not control these cookies and they are governed by each provider's own privacy and cookie policies.

  • Firebase (Google LLC): Powers account authentication and the gallery database. Sets authentication tokens and session cookies. Governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Analytics: Tracks page visits, session duration, and navigation flows to help us improve the platform. Governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google AdSense: Serves display advertisements. Uses cookies and device identifiers to show relevant ads based on your browsing behavior across the web. Governed by Google's Ads Policy.
  • Meta Audience Network: May serve ads on ExoraWorld. Sets cookies related to Meta's ad targeting systems. Governed by Meta's Privacy Policy.
  • rss2json.com: Processes our news RSS feed requests. Does not receive user-identifying data from ExoraWorld visitors and does not place cookies on visitor devices.
  • Google Fonts: Fonts are loaded from Google's servers. Google may log the request (IP address, browser) per their standard infrastructure logging. No user-identifying cookies are set for this.

Clicking a news article link or social media link takes you away from ExoraWorld. Once you are on a third-party site, their own cookie and privacy policies apply entirely. ExoraWorld has no visibility into or control over what those sites do.

Advertising & Personalized Ads

Advertising helps ExoraWorld stay free to use. We use Google AdSense and may use Meta Audience Network to display ads across the platform.

These networks may show you personalized ads — advertisements tailored to your interests based on your browsing history across many websites — or non-personalized ads that are based only on the page you're currently viewing, not your profile.

  • How personalization works: Google reads its own cookies (like IDE) to understand what topics interest you across the web, then matches those interests to advertisers bidding for ad space on ExoraWorld.
  • What ExoraWorld shares: We share the page URL and contextual signals with ad networks. We do not share your name, email, or order history with advertisers.
  • Opting out: You can turn off personalized ads entirely. Your options are in Section 8 below. If you opt out, you will still see ads — just not personalized ones.

Opting out of personalized advertising does not mean you will see no ads on ExoraWorld. Non-personalized, contextual ads may still appear. These are based solely on what the current page is about, not on your browsing history.

Your Rights

Depending on where you are located, you may have legal rights regarding how your data is collected and processed via cookies. These include:

  • Right to be Informed: This Cookie Policy exists to satisfy that right — you're reading it now.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: You can clear cookies and opt out of non-essential tracking at any time using the methods in Sections 7 and 8.
  • Right to Access: Contact us to ask what data has been collected about you via cookies or analytics tracking.
  • GDPR / UK GDPR (EEA & UK users): You have enhanced rights under European data protection law, including the right to object to processing based on legitimate interests and the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.
  • CCPA (California users): You have the right to opt out of the "sale" of personal information, which under CCPA may include sharing behavioral data with advertising networks. Use the opt-out links in Section 8.

ExoraWorld is operated from Bangladesh. We honor the rights listed above to the extent applicable to our operation. Contact us at the details in Section 11 to exercise any of these rights.
