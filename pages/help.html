FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Browse by topic or use the search above. If you don't find your answer here, use the contact form below.

Account

Head to the ExoraWorld homepage and click the Sign Up button in the top navigation. You can register using your Google account, Facebook account, or with an email and password. Account creation is completely free and takes under a minute.

Account

On the login screen, click Forgot Password? and enter your registered email address. A password reset link will be sent within a few minutes. Check your spam/junk folder if it doesn't appear. If you signed up via Google or Facebook, your password is managed by those services directly.

Account

Yes. You can browse the Gallery, read News, play Games, and view Content without an account. An account is required to access the full gallery (favorites, recently viewed), purchase from the Store, and save your game progress and leaderboard scores.

Account

To request account and data deletion, contact us at theexoraworld@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request". Include the email address associated with your account. We will process your request and confirm deletion within 30 days, as outlined in our Privacy Policy. Order records required by law may be retained for up to 2 years.

Store

Browse products in the Store, click the product you want, and follow the listed payment instructions. Send payment to the account details shown on the product page, then submit your payment proof (screenshot including transaction ID, amount, and date) through the form on that page. We verify payment and deliver your product within 1–12 hours.

Store

First, check your spam/junk folder — some email providers filter automated messages. If you still haven't received your product within 24 hours of payment confirmation, contact us at theexoraworld@gmail.com with your order reference and a clear screenshot of your payment proof. We will resolve it as a priority.

Store

Refunds are issued when: the product was never delivered after payment confirmation; the product delivered is significantly different from what was described; or a duplicate payment was made in error. Refunds are not available for change of mind, incorrect contact info provided, or products that have already been used. Requests must be submitted within 72 hours of order confirmation. See our full Terms & Conditions for details.

Payment

We accept: Bkash, Nagad, Cellfin, Bank Transfer (Bangladesh local methods) and AirTM, Skrill, Payoneer (international). The exact payment account details are listed on each individual product page in the Store. Always pay the exact listed amount and submit proof with your transaction ID, amount, date, and sender information.

Payment

Yes — payment accounts are listed directly on official ExoraWorld product pages only. We will never ask for your payment password, PIN, or OTP. Be cautious of impersonators using similar names. If you are ever unsure whether an account is official, contact us at theexoraworld@gmail.com before sending any payment.

Games

Yes — all browser games on ExoraWorld, including VoidRunner X, are free to play. Some games may offer premium features or bonus content available for purchase in the Store. No account is required to play, though signing in lets you save scores to the leaderboard and track your progress.

Games

Use the contact form below and select "Game Bug Report" as the topic. Please include: the game name, the device and browser you're using, a description of the bug, and steps to reproduce it if possible. Screenshots or screen recordings are very helpful. You can also report bugs directly on our Discord server.

Gallery

Images marked as free to download may be used for personal, non-commercial purposes. Premium images may require purchase and have specific usage rights stated on their listing page. You may not redistribute, resell, or commercially exploit gallery images without written permission. All gallery content is either original photography by ExoraWorld, verified public domain (CC0), or Creative Commons licensed — no copyrighted third-party material is hosted here. See our Terms for full details.

News

ExoraWorld is a news aggregator — we do not write news ourselves. We display headlines and brief excerpts from publicly available RSS feeds published by BBC News, IGN, and The Daily Star Bangladesh, parsed via the rss2json.com API. Every article links directly back to the original publisher. All news content remains the property of the respective publishers.

News

ExoraWorld aggregates news from third-party publishers and does not author, edit, or verify news content. Responsibility for accuracy lies solely with the original publisher. Click the article link to visit the source, and if the content is incorrect, report it directly to that publisher. ExoraWorld disclaims all liability for the accuracy of aggregated news content.

Legal

Email us at theexoraworld@gmail.com with the subject "DMCA Notice — [Your Name]". Include: your name and contact details, a description of the copyrighted work you claim has been infringed, the URL of the allegedly infringing content on ExoraWorld, and a statement under penalty of perjury that you are the rights holder or authorized agent. We will respond within 48 hours and take appropriate action. Full procedure is in our Terms & Conditions, Section 16.

Legal

We collect data to operate the platform, process orders, and serve advertising. Data is stored via Firebase (Google) and may be shared with advertising networks and analytics providers. Full details are in our Privacy Policy. You have the right to access, correct, or delete your data at any time — email us at theexoraworld@gmail.com with your request.

Business

ExoraWorld offers both programmatic advertising (via Google AdSense and other networks) and direct sponsorship placements for relevant brands. For direct advertising inquiries — including banner placements, newsletter sponsorships, or featured content — use the contact form below and select "Advertising Inquiry", or email us with your brand details, target audience, and campaign goals.

Business

Include a brief description of who you are, what you're proposing, and what value the collaboration offers to both sides. We review all proposals and respond within 5 business days.
