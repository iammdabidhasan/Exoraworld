News Aggregation — Open-Source RSS & Third-Party Attribution

ExoraWorld's News section is a news aggregator. We do not author, write, or create news content. We fetch and display publicly available news headlines, brief excerpts, and metadata from legitimate open-source RSS feeds provided by established media organizations.

News Sources We Aggregate

  • BBC News (bbc.co.uk) — via official public RSS: World news, Technology, Business, Science & Environment
  • IGN (ign.com) — via public RSS: Gaming and entertainment news
  • The Daily Star Bangladesh (thedailystar.net) — via official public RSS: Bangladesh national and regional news
  • Additional news sources may be added at any time via their officially available RSS feeds

How News Aggregation Works

  • We use the rss2json.com API service to parse publicly available RSS feeds into displayable content
  • We display only the article title, a brief description or excerpt, the source publication name, and the publication date
  • Every news item includes a direct link back to the original article on the publisher's own website
  • We do not store, copy, reproduce, or host full article text from any news source
  • All original news content remains the property of the respective publishing organization

Fair Use & News Aggregation: The display of news headlines, brief descriptions, and publication metadata for the purpose of directing users to original sources is widely recognized as fair use under copyright law (17 U.S.C. § 107 and equivalent international provisions) and is standard practice for news aggregators. ExoraWorld operates in full compliance with this principle. We do not reproduce full copyrighted articles. All attribution is clearly displayed. Source publications benefit from referral traffic generated through our aggregation.

ExoraWorld asserts no copyright claim over news content it aggregates. All news content is the property of the respective publishers. If you are a representative of a news organization with concerns about how your content is displayed, please contact us at the details in Section 25 and we will respond within 48 hours.
